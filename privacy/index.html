Day+

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Day+ is a habit tracker that keeps your days on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your habits.

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays in the app on your phone.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card.
  • If you write to us, we only use your email to answer.

Who we are

Day+ is made by Thony Adrian, an independent developer in Indonesia. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Day+. You can reach us at dayplushabit@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

Day+ saves the following in a database inside the app, on your iPhone only:

  • Your first name, if you type one (it's optional and only used to greet you and to sign your day cards).
  • Your habits: names, colours, notes, goals and units, reminder times and sounds, order, and whether a habit is paused or archived.
  • Your check-ins: which habit you did, on which day, how much, and at what time.
  • Time off: the days you put on vacation or paused a habit.
  • Your onboarding answers: the hours a day you think you lose to your phone, why you came to Day+, and the plan you chose. They're used to personalise a few screens and your recaps.
  • Your recap choices, like a habit to focus on or an intention for the month.
  • Your settings, like appearance, sounds, haptics and reminders.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We have no server that stores your data.

Backups

  • iCloud / iPhone backup. Your Day+ data is part of your iPhone's normal backup. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy. We can't access it.
  • Backup file and CSV export. When you export, Day+ creates the file on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. It only goes where you choose to send or save it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop).
  • Restore. When you restore, the file you pick is read on your phone and replaces the data in the app.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, Day+ schedules its reminders and recap notifications on your phone. They are not sent through our servers or a push service. Reminders show your habit's name, so it can appear on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Photos and sharing

Day cards and recap cards are drawn on your phone. If you tap Save image, Day+ asks permission to add the picture to your Photos. It can't see or read your photo library. If you share a card, it goes only where you send it. You can hide habit names on share cards in Settings › Privacy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are paid through your Apple ID and processed by Apple. We never receive your name, card or payment details from Apple.

To check which plan you have and to restore purchases, Day+ will use RevenueCat, a service that manages in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history from Apple, and basic device and app information (such as the iOS and app version and your App Store country). It does not receive your habits. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Crash reports

Day+ has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. If you've chosen in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements to share with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in iPhone Settings.

When you contact us

If you use Help & feedback, Day+ opens your Mail app with our address and the app and iOS version filled in. We receive what you choose to send, including your email address, and use it only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings › Your data › Delete all data to erase everything in the app, or delete the app from your phone. Backup files you exported are yours to delete. Because we don't hold your data, there is nothing on our side to delete; if you've emailed us, ask and we'll delete those emails.

Children

Day+ is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Wherever you live, including under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP) or the GDPR, you can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. In practice, the only personal data we may hold is an email you sent us. Write to dayplushabit@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also mention it in the app.
