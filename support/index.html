Quick answers

I got a new phone. Where's my plan?
Tap Restore Purchase on the plans screen or in Settings, using the same Apple ID.

Where are my habits?
They're on your phone and in your iPhone's iCloud backup. You can also export a backup file in Settings › Your data › Backup & restore, and restore it on another phone.

How do I cancel?
iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Day+. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
